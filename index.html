Support

Until Home keeps unsolved missing and murdered persons cases easy to find and hard to forget, until every one of them comes home.

We're David and Kris, a husband and wife team. The app was inspired by the Missing and Murdered Indigenous Women (MMIW) movement.

Have information about a case?

Please don't send case tips to us. Contact the agency listed on the case in the app, or call 911 in an emergency. BIA cases can be reported to the BIA Missing and Murdered Unit by texting BIAMMU to 847411 or calling 1-833-560-2065.

Something in a case looks wrong

Case details come from public sources: the FBI, the Bureau of Indian Affairs, and NamUs. Each case links to its original source, which has the most current information. If a case has been resolved or contains an error, let us know and we'll update or remove it.

My case boards aren't syncing

Case boards sync through your own iCloud account. Make sure you're signed in to iCloud on each device and that iCloud is turned on for Until Home in Settings. Pull down on the Case Boards list to refresh.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device. To remove case boards from iCloud as well, delete them in the app first, or go to Settings, then your name, then iCloud, then Manage Account Storage.
