Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

Until Home doesn't have accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking, and we don't sell or share your data. This page explains what the app does with information on your device.

Location

If you allow it, Until Home uses your location to:

  • find cases near you when you tap "Use Current Location" and show them on the map, and
  • send proximity alerts when you're near a case or enter a new city, if you turn alerts on and allow location access "Always."

Your location stays on your device. To look up cases, the app sends only a city and state to the case sources described below, never your exact location. Alert areas are set up and checked on your device by iOS. Apple's location services turn coordinates into a city name, under Apple's privacy policy.

You can change or turn off location access at any time in Settings, then Until Home, then Location.

Searches

When you search for a place or a name, the app sends that search to the sources it gets cases from so they can return results:

  • the FBI Wanted service,
  • the Bureau of Indian Affairs missing and murdered cases page,
  • the Until Home case database (NamUs cases), hosted by Supabase, and
  • Apple's podcast directory, for matching podcast episodes.

We don't keep a history of your searches. Like most web services, these services may briefly log technical details of each request, such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies.

The app remembers the cases you've recently viewed on your device only, so they can be added to case boards.

Case boards

Case boards, including pins, notes, dates, and pictures you add, are stored on your device and in your own private iCloud account so they sync across your devices. They're protected by your Apple Account. We can't see, access, or recover them.

When you add a picture, the app only receives the photo you choose. It doesn't access the rest of your photo library.

Notifications

Notifications, such as case alerts and the case of the day, are created on your device from cases you've searched. Until Home doesn't use a notification server or collect any information to send them. iCloud uses a silent background notification to keep your case boards in sync.

Links to other websites

Until Home links to case sources, podcasts, and our Patreon page. Those open in Safari and are covered by those websites' own privacy policies.

Crash reports

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. These don't identify you and help us fix problems.

Children

Until Home isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device. To remove case boards from iCloud, delete them in the app before removing it, or manage your iCloud storage in Settings.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we'll update this page and its effective date.
